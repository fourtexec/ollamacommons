OWN YOUR INFRASTRUCTURE

Your server.
Your rules.

Ollama Commons is a free, self-hosted directory—not a GPU provider. Add servers you run or have permission to list, and give the community a place to discover them.

See the setup ↓
servers.yaml
servers:
  - id: my-ollama
    name: My Ollama Server
    url: https://ollama.example.com
    region: US East
    free: true
    tags: [community, gpu]
↳ Replace the URL with your reachable Ollama API endpoint.
